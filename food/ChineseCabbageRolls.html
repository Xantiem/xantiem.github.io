Chinese cabbage rolls (白菜肉卷 Bái cài ròu juǎn)

Ingredients:

  • Nappa cabbage
  • Minced pork (or beef)
  • Spring onions
  • Grated ginger
  • Minced garlic
  • Soy sauce
  • Sesame oil
  • Black pepper
  • Chilli flakes (optional)

Method:

  1. Mix the filling ingredients together in a bowl.
  2. Blanch the cabbage leaves in boiling water until soft.
  3. Place meat filling in each leaf and roll.
  4. Steam or pan fry until fully cooked
